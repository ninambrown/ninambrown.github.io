I'm a second-year Astrophysics Ph.D. student at the University of Arizona working with Chad Bender. I'm broadly interested in visible/IR spectrograph design and applications for exoplanet detections and characterization. 

I'm currently working on reduce/reuse/recycling the retired spectrograph CSHELL, to use as the main nIR spectrograph for the upcoming Large Fiber Array Spectroscopic Telescope (LFAST).
